1. WHAT DOES "'₱[Amount] Protected Reserve (LOCKED)'" MEAN?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In standard Philippine commercial leasing (and in Pwestora Metrospace):
- When a tenant moves into a stall, they provide a Security Deposit equivalent 
  to two (2) months' rent (e.g., if monthly rent is ₱12,000, deposit is ₱24,000).
- "Protected Reserve": Indicates this money is held securely in an ESCROW fund 
  by the lessor. It is NOT being deducted, penalized, or spent.
- "LOCKED" Badge: Clarifies that neither the tenant nor the landlord can access 
  or consume this money for regular monthly rent payments.

WHO OWNS THIS MONEY?
- The ₱24,000 is 100% THE TENANT'S MONEY.
- The landlord does NOT own this money; they act only as an escrow custodian.
- When the lease terminates and the tenant vacates, the landlord MUST refund the 
  deposit back to the tenant within 30 days (less any actual physical damages).


2. THE TWO SEPARATE POTS OF MONEY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Understanding this difference is critical for both tenants and the defense panel:

  +-------------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
  | POT 1: SECURITY DEPOSIT ESCROW       | POT 2: MONTHLY OPERATING RENT        |
  | (e.g., ₱24,000 - Protected Reserve)  | (e.g., ₱12,000 - Due Each Month)     |
  +-------------------------------------+--------------------------------------+
  | • Paid once at lease signing.       | • Paid recurringly every month.      |
  | • Stays LOCKED in escrow.           | • This is what pays for occupancy.   |
  | • CANNOT be used to pay rent.       | • THIS IS THE ONLY MONEY RESTUCTURED |
  | • Refunded 100% when moving out     |   INTO INSTALLMENT MILESTONES.       |
  |   (if space has no damages).        |                                      |
  +-------------------------------------+--------------------------------------+


3. WHAT IS THE POINT OF THE INSTALLMENT / RESTRUCTURING PLAN?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A tenant realizes in Week 1 that they will struggle to pay the full ₱12,000 
lump sum due at the end of the month due to temporary cash flow issues (e.g., 
delayed inventory sales, supplier delays, or medical emergencies).

WITHOUT THE RESTRUCTURING PLAN:
- The tenant misses the due date.
- Compounding late penalty fees accumulate (₱500+).
- The tenant risks padlocking of the stall or eviction.
- The landlord loses a paying tenant and suffers long vacancy downtime.

WITH THE RESTRUCTURING PLAN:
- The tenant proactively selects a formal split schedule:
    * 2-Part Split (Bi-Weekly): 50% at month-end + 50% mid-next month.
    * 3-Part Split (Decennial): 33% at month-end + 33% mid-month + 33% month-end.
- Penalty fees are frozen ("Zero Late Fines").
- Landlord receives guaranteed, predictable cash flow without evicting.
- The tenant retains their stall and preserves business goodwill.


4. CAN THE "PROTECTED RESERVE" (SECURITY DEPOSIT) BE DEDUCTED?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DURING ACTIVE LEASE MONTHS:
- NO. It CANNOT be deducted to cover delayed monthly rent.
- Why? If a landlord allows rent to drain the deposit, the property is left 
  unprotected against sudden physical damage or utility abandonment.

AT THE END OF THE LEASE (WHEN VACATING):
- YES, but ONLY under three strictly audited conditions:
    1. Physical/Structural Damage: Broken tiles, damaged roll-up doors, shattered 
       fixtures beyond normal wear and tear.
    2. Unpaid Utility Bills: Outstanding electricity (Meralco) or water balances.
    3. Stall Abandonment: If the tenant flees without settling unpaid back rent.

WHAT IF THERE ARE NO DAMAGES?
- Deductions = ₱0.00.
- The full ₱24,000 is refunded to the tenant within 30 days.


5. WHERE IS THIS IN THE LEASE CONTRACT?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both policies are codified in the app's Lease Contract Screen 
(lib/screens/lease_contract_screen.dart):

Clause 1: Security Deposit
  "A security deposit equivalent to two (2) months' rent will be collected and 
   held. Refundable within 30 days of lease-end less deductions for damages."

Clause 2: Rent Restructuring Policy
  "Tenants facing temporary cash-flow difficulty may request to split monthly 
   rent into 2 or 3 installments subject to lessor approval. Late fees are frozen 
   provided agreed installment deadlines are strictly settled."


6. THESIS / CAPSTONE DEFENSE Q&A CHEATSHEET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: "Why show the Security Deposit on the Payment Restructuring screen?"
A1: "To provide transparency and legal clarity. When tenants request rent relief, 
     many mistakenly wonder if their security deposit is being forfeited or if 
     rent can be deducted from it. Showing 'Protected Reserve (LOCKED)' reassures 
     the tenant that their move-in escrow remains 100% safe, while reminding them 
     that restructuring is for monthly operating rent only."

Q2: "What prevents a tenant from abusing the installment plan?"
A2: "The request requires formal lessor review, an agreement confirmation modal, 
     a 48-hour cancellation cooling-off window, and logs directly into the lessor's 
     audit trail and official in-app chat. Consecutive failures trigger default 
     strikes as stipulated in the digital contract."

Q3: "Why not just deduct rent from the deposit when a tenant is short on cash?"
A3: "Under Philippine commercial leasing standards and Civil Code lease norms, 
     security deposits serve strictly as collateral for property damage and utility 
     liabilities upon move-out. Depleting it during tenancy leaves the lessor 
     vulnerable to risk."
